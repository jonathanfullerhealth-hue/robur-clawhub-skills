# Market Signals — Where Demand Is Forming Before It's Obvious

Prepared for Jonathan Fuller (BScKin, MScPT) — September 2026

Companion to `community-strategy.md` and `niche-analysis.md`. Those covered where the
market already is. This covers where it is heading, and how to keep finding that yourself.

**Caveat on evidence quality.** Vendor market-size reports (the "$X billion by 2034,
12.9% CAGR" genre) are marketing documents produced to sell reports. I have used them only
for direction, never as proof. The load-bearing numbers below are from surveys, polls,
clinical literature, and job postings.

## The method: six places demand shows up early

Most "trending niche" lists are lagging indicators — by the time a niche is listed, the
price is set and the seats are taken. These six patterns run ahead of that.

**1. Iatrogenic gaps — a new treatment creates a new problem.**
Adoption of a drug or procedure always outruns the support infrastructure around it. The
gap between "millions of people now do X" and "someone has built the protocol for X's side
effects" is usually 2–4 years wide, and it is the highest-value pattern on this list. It is
how the entire signal in section 1 below was found.

**2. Care-gap statistics in the clinical literature.**
When a paper reports "only 20% of patients received a referral to Y," that is a market gap
measured and published in a peer-reviewed number. Almost nobody outside the specialty reads
these. Search the literature for referral rates, adherence rates, and guideline-concordance
rates in any population you care about.

**3. Regulatory and standards deadlines.**
A new standard is forced spend on a known date. NFPA 1580 surfaced this way. Watch the
standards bodies and licensing colleges for whatever profession you sell to.

**4. Behaviour-versus-need divergence.**
Where a population's documented need runs opposite to its documented behaviour, there is a
market. Section 1 has the sharpest example I have seen this year.

**5. Job postings.**
What employers newly hire for leads training and education demand by six to twelve months.
A hospital system posting a role that did not exist two years ago is a signal.

**6. Scope-of-practice changes.**
When a college expands what a profession may do, a training market appears immediately,
because the permission arrives before the competence does.

## Signal 1: GLP-1 muscle preservation — take this one seriously

This is the strongest opportunity in this document, and it is almost purpose-built for your
credentials.

**The population went vertical.** 11% of US adults currently take a GLP-1 for weight loss,
up from **3% in 2024**. 15% have used one at some point. Prescription-based estimates put
roughly **12 million US adults** on a GLP-1 by end of 2026 — about 1 in 22.
([Gallup](https://news.gallup.com/poll/712157/glp-usage-reaches-new-high.aspx))

**The treatment causes the problem you solve.** An estimated **30–40% of weight lost on
GLP-1s is fat-free mass**, with lean mass declines of 10–15% in patients losing more than
15% of body weight. Older adults and anyone with sarcopenic obesity are most exposed.
([Lean Mass and Musculoskeletal Preservation in GLP-1-Based Obesity Treatment](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13303403/))

**The fix is exactly your profession.** Resistance training plus 1.2–1.6 g/kg protein. One
study combining GLP-1 with resistance training education and individualized protein saw
~13% body weight lost but only **3% muscle** lost at six months. Combining a GLP-1 with
supervised resistance and aerobic training has produced *gains* in lean mass — an outcome
the drug alone never delivers.

**And here is the divergence that makes it a market.** Among current users, **41% have
never done strength training at all**, and 17% have *decreased* it. The Endocrine Society
presented findings at ENDO 2026 that exercise *declines* among people on these medications.
Meanwhile only **20% of users report a referral to a dietitian**.
([PwC GLP-1 consumer trends](https://www.pwc.com/us/en/industries/consumer-markets/library/glp-1-consumer-trends.html),
[Endocrine Society](https://www.endocrine.org/news-and-advocacy/news-room/2026/maharjan-press-release-endo-2026))

Twelve million people are taking a drug that costs them muscle, four in ten have never
lifted anything, and one in five gets any structured support. That is not a soft trend. It
is a measured, published, growing care gap sitting directly on top of your licence.

### Sell it to the prescribers, not the patients

The consumer version is a $27 fitness product. The business version is not.

Med spas, weight-loss clinics, and primary care practices are prescribing GLP-1s at scale.
They have an outcome problem and a liability exposure, they are cash-rich, and **they cannot
staff an exercise professional.** Sell them the muscle-preservation protocol: assessment,
programming, monitoring, and the patient-facing materials — as a licensable system with your
name on the evidence.

That is your "rich people's problems" filter satisfied precisely: a wealthy buyer, an
expensive problem, and a credential they cannot fake internally.

**Your existing skills already are the product.** `training-plan-builder` generates the
protocol, `client-checkin-analyzer` is the monitoring layer, and
`research-breakdown-generator` produces the evidence content that sells it to clinicians who
will not buy on vibes.

**The risk:** this is a land rush, and pharma-adjacent players are moving. The window is
open now and will not be in eighteen months. Also expect scope questions — you program
exercise, you do not manage medication or write diets. Get the referral relationships right
and put the boundary in writing.

## Signal 2: Perimenopause and menopause programming

Specialized perimenopause and menopause programs are forecast to gain the **highest traction
of any specialization in 2026**, with industry bodies calling life-stage literacy a
must-have competency for trainers. The physiology backs it: up to 10% of bone mineral
density can be lost during perimenopause, with roughly 0.6% muscle loss per year afterward.
([NASM](https://www.nasm.org/resource-center/blog/top-fitness-trends),
[ACE](https://www.acefitness.org/resources/pros/expert-articles/9043/10-fitness-trends-in-2026-and-beyond/))

**The opening is coach education, not client coaching.** Demand is arriving faster than
competence — most trainers and coaches have no life-stage training at all. That is a
teachable, sellable gap and it maps onto your Fuller Health audience.

**The honest caveat:** you would be a man selling menopause programming. That is not
disqualifying — the physiology is physiology and you have the credential — but it is a real
trust cost. Mitigate it by partnering with a woman practitioner as co-lead rather than
fronting it alone.

## Signal 3: Longevity clinics as a channel

More than **700 dedicated longevity clinics** operated globally as of April 2026, and the
model is standardizing around annual memberships and physician-supervised healthspan
programs. These clinics do hire exercise physiologists — Northwestern Medicine's Human
Longevity Clinic has posted exactly that role.
([Research and Markets](https://www.researchandmarkets.com/reports/6225936/longevity-clinic-market-report),
[Northwestern Medicine](https://jobs.nm.org/job/chicago/exercise-physiologist-human-longevity-clinic-full-time-days/27763/78385852464))

**Stated honestly: I could not find quantitative evidence of an exercise-physiologist
staffing shortage in this sector.** I looked. The hiring is real; the shortage is an
inference, not a finding. Treat this as a distribution channel worth three phone calls,
not as a thesis worth a quarter.

## What I would do

**Signal 1 is the one.** It is the rare case where a large, fast-growing, well-funded
population has a clinically documented problem whose solution is precisely your licence,
with a published care gap proving nobody is serving it. Signals 2 and 3 are good. This one
is unusual.

**A cheap test, this month.** Call ten GLP-1 prescribers — med spas and weight-loss clinics
locally — and ask one question: *"What do you currently do about lean mass loss in your
GLP-1 patients?"* You are not selling. You are finding out whether they have an answer. If
most do not, you have found the gap in ten phone calls, and the answers write your offer
for you.

It also stacks rather than competes: practice owners are the community from
`niche-analysis.md`, and GLP-1 clinics are practice owners with an urgent, funded problem.

**Worth building:** a `market-signal-scanner` skill that runs the six patterns above on a
schedule — new standards in your verticals, care-gap statistics in the literature, relevant
job postings. You found NFPA 1580 and the GLP-1 gap by hand this week. That is automatable,
and it belongs in this repo alongside `lead-scanner`.
