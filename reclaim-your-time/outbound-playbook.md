# RYT Cold Outbound Playbook
**Model:** Nick Saraev — value-first, no-pitch, free audit → book. **Drives to:** the ROI Snapshot lead magnet.
**Placeholders:** `[First]` `[Clinic]` `[Observation]` `[SNAPSHOT_LINK]` `[BOOKING_LINK]` `[YourName]`

> Rules: short, specific, one CTA, no mechanism. You're giving value (a number, an audit), not
> pitching a service. Reply-or-click, never "hop on a call to learn about us."

---

## A. Cold email sequence (4 touches over ~9 days)

### Email 1 — the opener (Day 0)
**Subject lines (test 2–3):**
- `quick one, [Clinic]`
- `[First] — ~10 hrs/week hiding in [Clinic]?`
- `your front desk after the click`

**Body:**
```
Hi [First],

I looked at how [Clinic] handles [Observation — e.g. "new-patient inquiries from your site"]
and noticed the same gap I see in most [segment] practices: the work after the click
(follow-up, check-ins, content) quietly eats 8–12 hours a week.

I built a 60-second tool that shows exactly how many hours — and dollars — that's costing
you. No signup, no call: [SNAPSHOT_LINK]

If the number stings, I'll show you which piece to fix first.

[YourName]
(clinician, not an agency — I built this for my own practice first)
```

### Email 2 — the proof nudge (Day 3)
```
[First] — following up with the one stat that made me build this:

Clinics spending real money on ads still lose 30–40% of leads to slow follow-up.
Not an ads problem — a systems problem, after the click.

Your 60-second snapshot: [SNAPSHOT_LINK]
```

### Email 3 — the Loom (Day 6) — highest converting
```
[First], I recorded a 3-minute teardown of where I'd start with [Clinic] — [Observation].

[LOOM_LINK]

No pitch in it. If it's useful, the calendar's here: [BOOKING_LINK]
```

### Email 4 — the breakup (Day 9)
```
Last one, [First] — I'll get out of your inbox.

If reclaiming ~10 hours a week is ever worth 30 minutes, the door's open: [BOOKING_LINK]
Either way, the snapshot's yours free: [SNAPSHOT_LINK]
```

## B. Loom audit script (3–4 min, personalized)
The Loom is the wedge — a specific, free teardown of *their* practice. Structure:

1. **Open (10s):** "Hey [First], [YourName] — clinician who builds automation. Recorded a quick,
   honest look at [Clinic], no pitch."
2. **Show you did the work (45s):** screen-share their site / booking flow / one real gap.
   Name the specific thing: "I filled out your consult form and here's what happened next…"
3. **The cost (45s):** tie it to hours + dollars — pull up the Snapshot with their segment
   numbers. "For a practice your size that's ~X hours a year."
4. **One first move (45s):** name *what* you'd fix first — never *how*. "I'd start with your
   follow-up. That alone usually recovers a third of lost leads."
5. **CTA (15s):** "If you want the full map, book here — [BOOKING_LINK]. First 30 minutes are on me."

**Rules:** value not mechanism; one gap not ten; end on the calendar; keep it under 4 min.

---

## C. Targeting (from the demand audit)
- **Tier 1:** longevity / functional-med / concierge clinics, med spas (highest money × pain).
- **Observation sources:** their consult/booking form (test it), their social cadence (content
  gap), their reviews (complaints about wait/follow-up), job posts (hiring for admin = pain).
- **Volume:** Saraev's infra runs ~$400/mo (sending domains + a tool like Instantly/Apollo).
  Warm up domains before sending.

## D. Do-not rules
- Never reveal the "how" in email or Loom. The build is the paid part.
- Never attach a deck or price. The Snapshot and the free 30 min are the whole offer.
- One CTA per message. Personalize `[Observation]` — generic = deleted.
- Respect CAN-SPAM/CASL: real identity, physical address in footer, easy opt-out.
