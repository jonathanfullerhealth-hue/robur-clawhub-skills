# RYT Funnel Blueprint — Acquisition & Retention (AR-Funnel style)
**Emulates:** arfunnel.io's model — a coach/fitness-market **Acquisition & Retention** funnel: opt-in
landing → lead capture → email + SMS nurture → book-a-call, proof-heavy, benefit-led.
**Adapted for:** RYT's up-market clinic/med-spa buyer + the Saraev "free value, charge for build" rule.
**Note:** the specific demo.arfunnel.io page could not be loaded from this environment; this emulates the
platform's documented A&R model and the coach-funnel genre conventions, not a copy of one page.

## What we borrow vs. what we keep
| Borrow from AR Funnel | Keep from our (Saraev) model |
|---|---|
| Bold benefit hook + VSL slot | Free value up front; reveal value not mechanism |
| Two-step: engage → book | ROI Snapshot as the interactive lead magnet |
| Email **+ SMS** nurture (A&R) | Segment pricing; book on your Google Calendar |
| Proof-heavy layout | **No fabricated proof** — real credentials + real client results only |
| Light urgency (limited build slots) | Honest scarcity (you really do have limited capacity) |

## The funnel (steps)
```
1. TRAFFIC        cold email / Loom / social  →  the funnel landing page
2. HOOK           hero: the time-cost promise + a VSL slot (optional 90-sec Loom)
3. LEAD MAGNET    the interactive ROI Snapshot (engage, see hours + $)
4. CAPTURE        name + email (+ optional mobile for SMS) to "send my snapshot"
5. QUALIFY        3 quick questions (segment, # clients, biggest bottleneck)
6. BOOK           Google Calendar discovery call
7. NURTURE        A&R sequences below for anyone who captured but didn't book
8. CLOSE          call → quote a solution-template → build → retainer
```

## Landing page sections (order)
1. **Hero** — hook headline + subhead + primary CTA ("See what it's costing you").
2. **VSL slot** — 60–90-sec founder Loom (optional; placeholder until recorded).
3. **The Snapshot** — interactive calculator (the lead magnet), inline.
4. **How it works** — 3 steps: Snapshot → Working session → We build it.
5. **Proof** — founder credentials (real) + client-result slots (fill with REAL results only).
6. **Objections/FAQ** — "Is this just marketing?", "Will it replace my staff?", "What does it cost?"
7. **Final CTA** — book the call + honest scarcity ("we take a few builds a month").

## Copy bank (headlines / CTAs — AR energy, honest)
- **Hero H1:** "You didn't open a practice to answer emails."
- **Hero sub:** "See exactly how many hours — and dollars — manual admin is costing you. 60 seconds, no pitch. Built by a clinician."
- **Primary CTA:** "Show me what it's costing" → scrolls to Snapshot.
- **Post-Snapshot CTA:** "Get my snapshot + book my working session."
- **Scarcity line:** "We take on a handful of builds a month so each one ships fast."
- **Objection — 'just marketing?':** "No. We build the systems that run after the click — follow-up, check-ins, content — so your team stops doing them by hand."

## A&R nurture — EMAIL (for capture-but-didn't-book)
- **E1 (immediate):** deliver the snapshot number + one-line "here's the first thing I'd fix" + booking link.
- **E2 (Day 2):** the speed-to-lead / retention stat for their segment + booking link.
- **E3 (Day 4):** a 2-line mini case or a "day in the life on autopilot" + booking link.
- **E4 (Day 7):** soft breakup — "want me to just send the plan?" + booking link.

## A&R nurture — SMS (only if they gave a mobile + consented)
- **S1 (1 hr after capture):** "Hi [First] — Jonathan here (the clinician). Sent your time-cost snapshot to your email. Want the 15-min version live? [link]"
- **S2 (Day 3):** "[First] — the piece I'd automate first for [segment] is usually [lead follow-up]. Worth a quick look? [booking link]"
- SMS needs express consent (a checkbox at capture) + STOP-to-opt-out. Keep it to 2 touches.

## Retention side of "A&R" (post-client)
Once someone buys, the same funnel logic runs inward: onboarding sequence → monthly value email →
the `retention-engine` board keeps THEM from churning. Acquisition and Retention, one system.

## Honesty guardrails (do not cross)
- No invented testimonials, results, logos, or "as seen in." Proof slots stay empty until real.
- No fake countdown timers. If you show scarcity, it must be true.
- Reveal value, not mechanism, pre-sale. The build is the paid part.
- SMS/email: identity + address + opt-out; SMS requires express opt-in.
