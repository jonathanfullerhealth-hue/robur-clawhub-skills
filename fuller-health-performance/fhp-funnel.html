Common questions

Is this for athletes or regular people?

Both. The five domains matter whether you're chasing a 100-miler or just want to carry your own bags at 75. The plan scales to your goals.

I'm dealing with an injury or pain — can you help?

That's the physiotherapy side. We assess it properly and build training around it, not despite it. (Not a substitute for diagnosis of an acute medical issue — we'll tell you if you need one.)

What does coaching cost?

The scorecard and the assessment are free. Coaching is scoped to what you actually need — you'll know the plan and the price before you commit.

Do I have to be in-person?

No — assessment and coaching run remotely. The plan comes to you.
