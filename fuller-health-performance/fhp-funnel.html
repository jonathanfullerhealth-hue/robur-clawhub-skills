Straight answers

Common questions

I just want to lose weight — is this for me?

Yes. Weight loss and healthy living is one of the three programs. We build training and nutrition habits that hold in a real life — no crash resets, no punishing yourself.

I'm over 50 / getting back into it — too late?

Not remotely. The longevity program is built for active adults 50–90. Strength and your aerobic engine are trainable at any age, and they're what keep you capable.

Can you coach me for a race?

Yes — from a first 5K to an ultra. Your coach has run 200-mile races and programs road and trail runners for real goals.

Do I have to be in person?

No — assessment and coaching are 100% remote. The plan comes to you.

I've got a nagging injury — can you help?

We build training around it with an injury-prevention and exercise-therapy lens. This isn't a substitute for medical diagnosis of an acute problem — if you need one, we'll tell you.
