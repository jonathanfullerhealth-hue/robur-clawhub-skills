## The business
**Fuller Health & Performance (FHP).** A free 60-second online "scorecard" is the lead
magnet: a person picks a goal, rates themselves on five domains (strength, aerobic
engine, recovery, nutrition, movement), and instantly sees their score + their "weak
link" — the one thing capping their goal. It's free to see the score; email is only
asked to book a free consult. Live at: https://fuller-health-performance.vercel.app

## Who we help / problem / solved (must be obvious in every asset)
- WHO: (1) someone carrying weight, tired of crash resets; (2) an active adult 50–90 who
  wants to stay capable; (3) a runner chasing a 5K → ultra.
- PROBLEM: they don't know their weak link, so effort scatters and nothing holds.
- SOLVED: 60 seconds names the weak link → free consult turns it into a plan that fits
  their life → a pro coaches the long game.

## Three programs
1. Weight loss & healthy living — habits that hold, no punishing resets.
2. Longevity — for active adults 50–90; strength + aerobic engine are trainable at any age.
3. Running coaching — first 5K to ultra.
